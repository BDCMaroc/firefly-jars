Common questions

The shelf jammed, what now? Fireflies light dots from the bottom of the picture up. Tap jars whose color can reach an open dot first, and keep at least one shelf slot free.

Is my progress saved? Yes, automatically on your device.

Sound or music too loud? Tap the speaker button at the top of the screen. The game also follows your phone's silent switch.
