Honey Flight: Bee Puzzle

A cozy, colorful puzzle for iPhone. Tap a honey jar, watch the bees fly out, and help them carry every honey cube home to clear the picture. (Formerly Firefly Jars.)

Common questions

The hive jammed, what now? Bees can only reach cubes from the outside of the picture. Send jars whose color is on the edge first, and keep a spot free on the pad. Boosters (hammer, bomb, extra slot, shuffle) can help.

What are connected jars? On hard levels, two jars joined by a bar leave together, so you need two free spots on the pad.

How do I unlock more levels? Levels 1–20 are free. A one-time purchase unlocks all levels and endless play. Already bought it? Tap Restore Purchase in Settings.

Is my progress saved? Yes, automatically on your device.

Sound or music too loud? Turn them off separately in Settings. The game also follows your phone's silent switch.
