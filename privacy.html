Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

The short version

Honey Flight: Bee Puzzle (formerly Firefly Jars) does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, stars, boosters, daily rewards and sound settings are saved only on your iPhone so the game remembers where you left off. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The optional "Full Game" in-app purchase is processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Whether you bought it is checked with Apple on your device so the game can unlock the extra levels.

Advertising

This version contains no advertising. If a future version adds ads, this policy will be updated before that version is released and will explain what the ad provider collects and how to opt out.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: contact support.
